# One-way print/PDF output

Open a complete saved calculated revision and choose **Print / Save PDF** in the Visual Flight Log panel. The browser prints that panel as it appears when the action is invoked. Its displayed values, row order, warnings, and weather statements are preserved. Open disclosures are included with their current contents; collapsed disclosures, including their summaries, are omitted. The action does not print the route editor, aircraft editor, or Calculation Inspector, and does not expand disclosures or apply unsaved editor values.

The action requires a complete saved calculated revision and all referenced weather evidence. A draft, preview, infeasible calculation, or revision missing weather evidence cannot be printed. The output is generated entirely in the browser; it does not refresh weather or upload planning data. This is a teaching and planning aid, not an official weather briefing or complete preflight plan. There is no PDF import path.

In the browser print dialog, choose **Save as PDF**, landscape paper, and disable browser-supplied headers and footers if a clean worksheet is preferred. The browser controls the PDF filename and destination.
